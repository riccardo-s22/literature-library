- Study type: Pre-clinical, interventional, murine model of TBI.

# What is actually new?
- Biological novelty: First-in-class demonstration that a dried platelet biologic can repair the BBB and reduce neuroinflammation after TBI via Ang-1/Tie2 signaling.
- Methodological novelty: Freeze-drying of platelets as a scalable therapeutic approach; mechanistic dissection using Tie2 inhibition.
- Dataset or resource novelty: Cortical and hippocampal transcriptome from murine TBI treated with FDPlt — if deposited, a useful reference dataset.
- What was already known: Ang-1/Tie2 pathway is a central regulator of endothelial stability. BBB breakdown after TBI initiates harmful cascades. Platelets are known mediators of hemostasis; fresh platelets have been explored in TBI.

# Strengths
- Design strength: Mechanistic interrogation of Tie2 pathway via inhibitor experiment; multiple independent endpoints (hemorrhage, perfusion, BBB permeability, transcriptomics, neuroinflammation markers).
- Validation strength: Tie2 inhibition experiment provides causal mechanistic support for Ang-1 as the key mediator.
- Reproducibility strength: Unknown — group sizes and replicate numbers not confirmed.
- Translational or ecological realism: In vivo murine model; clinically relevant FDPlt biologic (Cellphire Therapeutics affiliation noted in author list). FDPlts are a freeze-dried, storable product with translational potential.
