# What is actually new?
- Biological novelty: First reported PET-compatible small-molecule tracer targeting TDP-43 aggregates with demonstrated in vivo brain penetrance and target engagement
- Methodological novelty: Combined SPR + fluorescent labeling + ARG + multi-species PET characterization workflow for TDP-43 tracer development
- Dataset or resource novelty: AAV-hTDP43 disease mouse model validated as in vivo test system for TDP-43 tracer
- What was already known: TDP-43 PET was an unmet need; small-molecule binders to TDP-43 aggregates existed but no PET-ready tracer with demonstrated brain penetrance

# Strengths
- Design strength: Multi-species PET (rat + NHP) provides pharmacokinetics in CNS-relevant models; ARG validates tissue binding in human material
- Validation strength: SPR affinity + multiple in vivo models + human tissue ARG is a thorough preclinical package
- Reproducibility strength: Industrial (J&J) discovery pipeline implies GMP-grade radiochemistry infrastructure
- Translational or ecological realism: NHP data is the highest preclinical relevance for human PET; human tissue ARG bridges to clinical

# Limitations and bias risks
- Sample-size or power limitation: Animal numbers not reported in abstract; likely small n typical for PET tracer studies
- Confounding: AAV-hTDP43 model may not replicate all conformational variants of disease TDP-43
- Batch or site effects: Single lab (J&J) preclinical development
- Pseudoreplication risk: Unknown without full text
- Leakage or overfitting risk: Not applicable (imaging study)
- Generalizability limitation: No human imaging data; translational gap between preclinical and clinical PET is substantial; radiotracer pharmacology may differ in diseased vs healthy human brain
- Missing controls: Human brain PET not reported; selectivity over full range of clinical co-pathologies unknown
- Missing validation: Human first-in-human PET study not reported; dosimetry not reported
- Author-stated limitations: None visible without full text inspection
- Additional limitations identified during review: Tracer developed by J&J (conflict of interest for commercial development); LATE and AD TDP-43 co-pathology not directly tested in vivo
