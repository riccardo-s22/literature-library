# What is actually new?
- Biological novelty: Defined shock-wave insult → chronic, age-accelerated neurodegeneration in a genetic model, with Tau/PTL-1 cytoskeletal involvement.
- Methodological novelty: Two calibrated exposure platforms (free-field-like SWG + clinical rESWT) for tractable br-mTBI.
- What was already known: Blast mTBI links to chronic symptoms; mechanisms linking acute insult → progressive dysfunction underdefined.

# Strengths / Limitations
- Strengths: Calibrated, reproducible exposure; lifespan phenotyping; mechanistic tractability; cross-platform replication.
- Limitations: Preprint (not peer-reviewed); invertebrate model (strong transferability caveat to mammalian/human TBI); quantitative parameters and statistics not yet inspected; group sizes unknown.
